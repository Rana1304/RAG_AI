# Ordering and Customer Service Policy

## Placing an Order
1. The customer chooses menu items and quantities.
2. Staff confirms availability, price and requested customization.
3. Staff calculates the final total, including authorized add-ons.
4. The customer confirms the order.
5. Payment and preparation follow the food cart's actual procedures.

## Customization
Customers may request supported changes such as less spice, no onion, extra cheese or additional toppings. Add-on prices must be confirmed from the current menu.

## Out-of-Stock Items
Tell the customer that the item is unavailable. Suggest similar items that are listed in the knowledge base. Do not claim an item is available without confirmation.

## Cancellations and Refunds
The actual cancellation, refund and replacement policies have not been supplied. Ask staff to confirm the policy; do not promise a refund or replacement.

## Bulk Orders
For large orders, collect the requested items, quantities, required time and contact details if needed. Staff must confirm the final quotation and preparation time.

## Complaints
Respond politely, acknowledge the concern and refer payment disputes, food quality complaints or refund requests to the owner or responsible staff member.

## Discounts
Only apply offers explicitly approved by the business. Never invent coupon codes or discounts.

## Order Confirmation Template
Order items: Confirmed items and quantities.
Subtotal: Calculate from verified prices.
Add-ons: List confirmed extras.
Total: Confirmed payable amount.
Status: Pending staff confirmation until accepted by the business.
